Subject: Monthly active-contributor counts for citizen-science thesis

Hello,

I am studying how active participation has changed over time across Zooniverse,
Eterna, EyeWire, and Foldit for my sub-thesis.

Could you provide a platform-wide monthly series of distinct accounts completing
at least one scientific task, from the earliest available period to the latest
complete month? Please include the period boundaries/timezone, task definition,
coverage gaps, and any changes to account or task counting.

Annual distinct contributor counts, supplied separately, would also be useful.
If an event export is more suitable, stable pseudonymous account and event IDs,
timestamps, and scientific task type/status would suffice; names and contact
details are not needed.

It would help to distinguish tutorials/practice from scientific contributions
and clarify how anonymous, staff/test/bot, deleted/spam, or migrated accounts
are handled. My historical reference is Strasser et al. (2023),
https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0293289, which reports a 30-day active-user
snapshot in June 2021. I would like to establish whether an updated time series
with compatible definitions is available.

We will acknowledge the source and follow the applicable data-use terms.

Thank you.
